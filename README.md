This projects contains the R scripts I developed and used during my M2 internship at the National Museum of Natural History of Paris (France) in the DIVA team of the ISYEB research lab. 
This internship aim was to investigate humpback whale's world-wide genomic structure and recent demography thanks to whole genome sequencing data.
The internship was supervised by Stefano Mona (PI) and Maël Le Gouellec (PhD). 
The scripts were written by me based on some S. Mona previous work and custom functions.
